Questions

When will it arrive?

Orders ship in 2–3 business days, and most US addresses get them 2–5 days after that. You’ll get tracking by email.

What if it doesn’t fit?

Exchange it for another size free within 30 days. Reply to your order email and tell us the size you want.

How does the 15% off work?

Put two or more items in your bag, any designs or sizes, and each one drops by 15%. The bag shows the new price right away.

Can I pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay?

Yes. Checkout is run by Stripe and takes Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link and all major cards.
